# Mottu

> Mottu is the leading motorcycle-rental ecosystem in Latin America, founded in 2020 in Sao Paulo, Brazil. It rents motorcycles to gig and delivery couriers who lack access to traditional credit, bundling insurance, maintenance, 24-hour support and financial services (Mottu Pay), and offers white-labeled last-mile delivery (Mottu Entregas) plus fleet-management software across Brazil and Mexico. Mottu operates as a consumer and B2B mobility/fintech app and does not publish a public developer API.

## About
- Sector: fintech / mobility / last-mile logistics
- Founded: 2020, Sao Paulo, Brazil (founder Rubens Zanelatto)
- Markets: Brazil and Mexico (100+ cities)
- Backed by: QED Investors (raised US$150M+)
